3. Third-party services

4. Data retention

BudgetWise retains user data until it is deleted by the user or removed under account closure workflows. When an account is permanently deleted, application data associated with that account is removed from the primary database, subject to any limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, or backup restoration windows.

5. User rights

  • Users can request access to the data stored about them.
  • Users can export their data through the settings page.
  • Users can permanently delete their account and owned data.
