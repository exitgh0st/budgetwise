1. Read `PROJECT-STATUS.md`
2. Present a concise summary using whatever numbers PROJECT-STATUS.md actually reports (do NOT hardcode a ticket count):

   - Current phase
   - Last completed ticket
   - Next ticket
   - Known issues (if any)
