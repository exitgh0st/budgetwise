# Landing Page

## Purpose

Public marketing homepage at `/` for guests.

## Key Behavior

- Highlights tracking, budgets, scheduled transactions, reports, and the AI advisor
- Shows a dashboard preview and conversion CTAs
- Redirects authenticated users to `/dashboard`
- Redirects signed-in but unverified users to `/verify-email`
